# Trading Docks plan tiers

## Limits

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Deck Vault | Inventory |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Free | $0 | $0 | 10 decks | No access |
| Collector | $4.99 | $49 | 50 decks | 10,000 items |
| Seller | $19.99 | $199 | Unlimited | 50,000 items |
| Store | $49.99 | $499 | Unlimited | 250,000 items |

Deck creation and imports are blocked when a limited plan reaches its
allowance. Collector inventory additions are blocked before they would exceed
10,000 physical units. Existing data remains visible after a downgrade so a
customer can delete or export records before upgrading.

The permanent owner account retains its real subscription while using Admin
Plan Preview. Free has Deck Vault access but does not have Inventory access.
