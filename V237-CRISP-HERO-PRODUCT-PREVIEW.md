# Trading Docks v237 — Crisp hero product preview

## Readability fixes

- Removed continuous 3D rotation and floating transforms from the complete
  dashboard surface. Text is no longer rasterized on a moving subpixel layer.
- Increased metric values to 18px extra-bold tabular numerals.
- Raised labels, activity text, chart labels, badges, and helper text to
  readable sizes.
- Replaced translucent surfaces with more opaque navy panels.
- Increased border contrast and reduced stacked blur effects.
- Brightened chart bars, grid structure, and active values.
- Enlarged and strengthened the search control.
- Kept motion on ambient light, status indicators, and chart bars instead of
  moving text-bearing components.
- Strengthened floating status widgets while keeping them stationary and crisp.
