# Deck Vault Fluid Board — v131

The Show Off Your Deck poster now uses one cohesive deck-board composition
inspired by physical tournament deck displays.

## Improvements

- Square exports use up to seven category columns so common Commander decks
  form one balanced, full-width board.
- Instagram portrait and Story exports automatically reflow to five and four
  columns.
- Category panels no longer surround cards with large, visually heavy boxes.
- Cards use more of the available canvas and align to one consistent ratio.
- Every duplicate copy is visibly layered in its category stack.
- Category labels are compact, consistent, and easy to scan.
- The Trading Docks wordmark is larger and remains outside the deck area.
- Pure Black, Harbor Cyan, Midnight Foil, and Color Identity themes remain.

No Supabase migration or environment-variable change is required.
