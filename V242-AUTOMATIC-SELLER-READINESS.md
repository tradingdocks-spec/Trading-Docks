# Trading Docks v242 — Automatic Seller Readiness

The Seller Launch Center now verifies durable Supabase data automatically.

## Automatically verified

- Active workspace
- Inventory records and total units
- Ready marketplace connections
- Saved Mana Pool self-service credentials
- Imported marketplace orders
- Completed marketplace sync runs
- CRM customers in the active workspace

## Manual until durable persistence exists

- Purchasing/buying-rule configuration
- Automation review

These are intentionally labeled manual rather than falsely claiming an
automatic verification that the underlying product cannot yet support.
