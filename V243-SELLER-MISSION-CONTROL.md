# Trading Docks v243 — Seller Mission Control

## Personalized greeting

Seller and Store users can set a business or store name under:

Settings → Workspace → Business or store name

Mission Control greets the account with that value. When it is blank, it falls
back to the saved full name or the email username.

## Mission Control

The Seller and Store dashboard now includes:

- Personalized time-aware greeting
- Business health score
- Revenue, profit, order, inventory, and customer metrics
- Best next action
- Marketplace health
- Alerts
- Business timeline
- Seller readiness
- Quick actions

Collectors and Free accounts retain the existing modular collection dashboard.
