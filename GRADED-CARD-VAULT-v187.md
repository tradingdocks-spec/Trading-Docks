# Trading Docks v187 — Graded Card Vault

- Renames the PSA Slab Vault to the Graded Card Vault throughout Inventory.
- Requires the trading card game when filing a new graded card.
- Includes Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Disney Lorcana, One Piece, Flesh and Blood, Star Wars: Unlimited, Digimon, Dragon Ball Super, Sports / Non-TCG, and Other.
- Adds grading-company selection for PSA, BGS, CGC, SGC, TAG, and Other.
- Keeps existing PSA records visible and compatible.
- Displays the TCG and grading company on each graded-card record and includes both in vault search.
- No new Supabase SQL is required.
