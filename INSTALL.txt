CSV CONVERSION ENGINE
- Added under a new Tools section in the sidebar.
- Seller and Store plans can access the tool.
- Converted cards can be saved to an existing inventory location.
- Users can create a new numbered CSV Bulk Box from the converter.
- Batches can remain unlisted or be marked Active on TCGplayer, eBay,
  Mana Pool, Trading Docks, or In-Store.

  marketplace listing allocations, and movement history.
