# V294 — Casual Freeform Playmat

- Replaced the seven-step phase tracker with one clear **Next turn** action.
- Next turn automatically untaps permanents, clears floating mana, resets the land play, and draws a card.
- Battlefield cards can be dragged freely around one continuous playmat on desktop or touch devices.
- Lands still tap and untap with a short click or tap and continue feeding the live mana pool.
- Kept subtle suggested land and permanent areas without locking cards into rigid rows.
- Preserved casting, Auto-pay, summoning sickness, graveyard, exile, command zone, mulligan, and manual draw tools.
