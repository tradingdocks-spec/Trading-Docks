# Card Shows Persistence — v91

- Card Show events can be deleted from Upcoming Shows or the event calendar.
- The Buying Rules card now has editable percentages for both Singles and
  Sealed Products.
- Buying-cart lines, quantities, actual paid amount, purchase date, seller, and
  notes remain saved after navigation, refreshes, or later searches.
- The buying cart clears only after a successful inventory purchase or when
  staff manually remove its items.
