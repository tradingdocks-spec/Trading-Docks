# Inventory Command Center release

## Plan availability

- Free: Inventory is unavailable and displays a plan comparison gate.
- Collector: Personal collection filing, storage locations, put-away, search, and movement history.
- Seller: Collector tools plus sales-channel summaries, listing allocations, operational action queues, pricing needs, and listing exceptions.
- Business: Seller tools plus listed value, cost basis, potential profit, saved operational views, inventory aging, advanced location metrics, and activity recovery controls.

## Inventory improvements

- Collapsible onboarding header for daily operational use.
- Physical units, available inventory, listed value, potential profit, and exception metrics.
- Sales-channel summaries with quantity/value and location filtering.
- Inventory action queue for put-away, listing, pricing, allocation, marketplace, and location exceptions.
- Business saved views for recently acquired, unlisted, multi-channel, high-value, missing-cost, missing-location, and marketplace-error inventory.
- Business inventory aging filters from 0–30 through 180+ days.
- Storage cards now show cost basis, available/listed quantities, unique items, activity, capacity, and warnings.
- “Unconfigured” renamed to the actionable “Needs setup.”
- Empty duplicate-location center reduced to a compact health banner.
- Improved activity history empty state and Business recovery affordances.
