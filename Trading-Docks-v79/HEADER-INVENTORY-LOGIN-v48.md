# Trading Docks v48

## Included

- Reliable 30-day Remember Me session handling and session verification
- Editable owned inventory quantity in the card-details drawer
- Inventory-cap and active-listing safeguards for quantity changes
- Functional, tier-aware Create menu
- Functional notification dropdown with read-state controls
- Workspace menu with plan, limits, pricing, billing, settings, and owner admin access
- Profile menu with profile, preferences, security, help, and sign out

## Workspace menu design

The Trading Docks dropdown owns workspace and subscription actions. The initials
dropdown owns personal account actions. This prevents duplicated settings and
keeps billing/store controls separate from the signed-in person's profile.
