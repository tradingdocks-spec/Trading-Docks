# Deck Story Social Showcase — v150

## What changed

- Replaced the rigid full-card columns with a balanced, modular Deck Story layout.
- Added a commander-led header with deck name, format, card count, and unique-card count.
- Included every deck card as a readable artwork row with name and quantity.
- Automatically balances categories across two or three columns.
- Keeps one- and two-card categories compact instead of stretching them.
- Added a Facebook/feed 4:5 preset alongside square and story exports.
- Improved the HD canvas export to match the on-screen preview.
- Changed deck value to opt-in so shared graphics do not expose it by default.
- Updated social sharing copy and export naming.

## Deployment

No Supabase migration is required.
