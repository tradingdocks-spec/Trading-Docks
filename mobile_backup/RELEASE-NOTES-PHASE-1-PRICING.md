# Trading Docks Mobile — Phase 1 Pricing Polish

## Updated membership structure

- Free — $0
  - 500 cards
  - 5 decks
  - Card scanner access
- Collector — $4.99/month or $49.99/year
  - Unlimited collection management
  - Financial insights and price history
  - Storage locations, trade binder, and wishlist
- Seller — $14.99/month or $149.99/year
  - Deal Desk
  - Card-show workflows
  - Sealed evaluator
  - Trading Docks web workspace
- Store — $49.99/month or $499.99/year
  - Store Deal Desk
  - Employee access
  - Shared workflows and permissions
  - Trading Docks web workspace
